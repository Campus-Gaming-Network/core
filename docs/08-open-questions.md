# 08 — Open questions

Decisions to resolve before or during implementation. Until answered, implementers and LLMs should **ask** rather than invent.

## Still open

### Profiles & affiliations

1. **Multiple majors** — Free-text tags, or a curated majors list?
2. **Graduate students** — Separate degree level field? Different verification rules?
3. **Faculty who are not students** — Profile fields beyond staff/faculty grant (title, department)?
4. **Alumni** — Auto-flip after graduation date, or manual affiliation?
5. **What else on profiles?** — Gaming IDs (Discord, Steam, Riot), pronouns, hometown, graduation year vs date, campus, transfer student flag?

### Events

6. **Approved organizers** — Who approves badge organizers, and is approval per-school or global?
7. **Past event “minor corrections”** — Exact allowlist of editable fields?
8. **Private unlock session** — Cookie/session length after password modal success? Rate-limit / lockout policy?
9. **Tournament capacity** — **Decided:** the entrant cap counts entries, so teams in a team tournament ([01](./01-product.md#tournaments)).
10. **Custom event banners (later)** — Moderation workflow when user uploads return?

### Teams & clubs

11. **Sponsored teams vs university programs** — Clubs are student clubs or university programs, and neither implies endorsement. How does a school-sponsored team display without reading as a university program?
12. **Club request workflow** — **Decided:** officers claim or request a club with evidence (an official club contact or the university's organization page; a school email alone is not enough), and site admins review in the Admin Console. See [01 — Product](./01-product.md#clubs).
13. **Who assigns teams to clubs** — **Decided:** not in the first clubs release; assigning teams to clubs is later.

### Tournaments

14. **Bracket / results** — **Decided:** registration and an external bracket link ship first; native single-elimination brackets and organizer-entered results follow, then round robin and Swiss, then double elimination ([01](./01-product.md#tournaments)).
15. **Tournament tied to event** — **Decided:** a tournament belongs to exactly one tournament-type event and shows on that event's page, with no separate tournament page ([02](./02-domain-model.md#tournament)).

### Content & safety

16. **Rich text** — **Decided:** plain text with newlines (see the table below).
17. **Support ticket fields** — Which fields are required on the public form?

### Technical

18. **Go API style** — **Decided:** REST/JSON behind the BFF ([11](./11-implementation-decisions.md)).
19. **Analytics** — Plausible vs Cloudflare Web Analytics vs other?
20. **Activity log vs audit log** — **Decided for events:** the event activity log reads `audit_logs`, with no second table ([02](./02-domain-model.md#event-activity-log)). Still open for a per-user activity history: one table with `kind`, or two tables?
21. **Friends / i18n** — timing and depth after the first release? (Notifications are decided: an in-app inbox; see [01](./01-product.md#notifications--email).)
